4. Speak about real people

TRY TO DO ORALLY. Use the sentence frame if necessary.

About you

I am quite ______.
I am not very ______.

Example: I am quite shy, but I am also patient.

Your friend

My friend is ______ because he/she ______.

Example: My friend is reliable because he always helps me.

Your family

My ______ is very ______.

Example: My brother is stubborn. He always wants to do things his own way.

Choose

What is more important in a friend?

honest / sociable / caring / reliable / patient

Example: I think a good friend should be honest and reliable.
